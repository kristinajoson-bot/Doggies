Questions People Ask

Is FurSteps a charity?

No. FurSteps is a workplace wellbeing program that companies pay for. Giving to the shelter is built into the price, and we publish exactly how much: at least 15% of what companies pay.

Do my staff need a fitness tracker?

No. FurSteps reads step counts from Apple Health on iPhone and Health Connect on Android. Steps typed in by hand don't count, to keep it fair.

Can employers see individual steps?

No. Employers only see team totals and bowls filled. Staff choose whether their name shows on leaderboards.

Which shelter do the bowls go to?

We're choosing our first partner shelter now and will name them here once we've agreed. All FurSteps companies feed the same shelter, so the total grows faster.

When can we start?

Our first workplace challenges start in early 2027. Join the early access list above and we'll be in touch about a pilot.

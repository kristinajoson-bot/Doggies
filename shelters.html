What the Shelter Gets

Regular support

At least 15% of what every FurSteps company pays goes to our partner shelter, all year round.

The shelter's choice

Dog food delivered to the door, or money for vet bills and care. The shelter decides what helps most.

New supporters

The shelter's dogs appear in the app, so workplace teams get to know them and cheer them on.

What We Ask of Shelters

  • A few photos and short stories about dogs in care, with permission to use them.
  • A quick note to confirm what was received, so companies can see the proof.
  • An occasional update we can share with walkers in the app.
  • No fees to join. Ever.
